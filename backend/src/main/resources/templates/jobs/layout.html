How the score works: the share of an occupation's typical tasks multiplied by how much of each today's AI tools can do. It is a catalog model, not a labor-market forecast. A high score means those tasks will change, not that the job disappears. Your personal score adds up to ±30 points from 11 questions about how you work.

Sources: occupation and task data from O*NET 29.3, U.S. Department of Labor / ETA, CC BY 4.0. Task time shares and observed AI use from the Anthropic Economic Index, CC BY. Human exposure labels from Eloundou et al. (2024), Science. Automation estimates combine a Claude rating with those labels.
